01 / About

Medicine is where I work.
Systems are what I study.

My career has moved through clinical medicine, healthcare financing and back into specialist medical training. That journey shaped a conviction: better healthcare requires better institutions, incentives, infrastructure and tools.

I am a Nigerian medical doctor training in anaesthesia at Aminu Kano Teaching Hospital. My professional path includes earlier experience in healthcare financing and HMO operations, giving me exposure to healthcare from both the clinical and organisational sides.

My clinical interests include perioperative care, regional anaesthesia, pain management, patient safety and the practical challenges of delivering reliable care in resource-constrained environments.

Beyond clinical practice, I build educational resources and explore how software, data and sound institutional design can improve healthcare delivery. I am particularly interested in the infrastructure required to make high-quality care more accessible across Africa.
