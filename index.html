Background

Clinician. Educator. Builder.

Trained across federal tertiary institutions in Nigeria, I apply first-principles reasoning to clinical practice and knowledge production with equal rigor. My clinical domain is anaesthesia; my long-horizon specialization target is pain management.

Beyond the operating theatre, I run a structured 15-week study cohort for WACS Diploma in Anaesthesia candidates, produce high-density SBA question banks and reference texts, and am developing anaesthesia.ng as a free-access education platform for trainees across West Africa.

Epistemologically, I am grounded in Popperian falsifiability, Deutschian optimism, and Talebian robustness: problems are solvable, knowledge grows by conjecture and criticism, and fragility must be designed out of systems.
